## 灵录 0.3.5

- 在「关于」页即可检查和下载更新。下载时显示进度，完成后提示重启。
- 「关于」页可设置更新下载方式。选择「自动」后，发现新版会自动下载。默认先询问。
- 从 0.3.3 或 0.3.4 升级，请先使用菜单栏的「检查更新」。

适用于 macOS 13.3+、Apple Silicon。

## English

- Check for and download updates from About. Capwise shows download progress and prompts you to restart when the update is ready.
- Choose your update download preference in About. Select Automatic to download new releases as they become available. Capwise asks first by default.
- To upgrade from 0.3.3 or 0.3.4, choose Check for Updates in the menu bar.

Requires macOS 13.3+ on Apple Silicon.
