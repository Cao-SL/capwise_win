## 灵录 0.3.10

- 新用户默认使用真人摄像头和圆形画中画。修改出镜方式或形状后，下次录制沿用上次选择。
- 录制前始终显示摄像头工具栏。悬浮的虚拟形象和真人预览都可拖动，选菜单时窗口不会跟着移动。
- macOS 从只读位置运行时，在线更新会把新版本安装到「应用程序」并重新打开。

## English

- New recordings default to a real camera and circular picture-in-picture. Your chosen camera mode and shape carry over to the next recording.
- Camera controls stay visible before recording. Drag either the avatar or camera preview without interfering with menu selections.
- On macOS, updating from a read-only location installs the new version in Applications and reopens it.
