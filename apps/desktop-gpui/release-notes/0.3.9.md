## 灵录 0.3.9

- 摄像头可以换成猫、熊或狐狸。表情跟踪在本机完成，选择卡通时，摄像头轨只保存卡通画面。
- 录制前可在摄像头工具栏切换画中画、左屏右人或左人右屏。左右分屏可以保留完整屏幕画面，圆形摄像头样式也会带入编辑器。
- 可将已连接的本地 AI 助手模型设为字幕和写稿等功能使用的文本模型。
- 「关于」页支持直接检查、下载和安装更新；安装包补齐了内置壁纸。

适用于 macOS 13.3+、Apple Silicon。

## English

- Replace your camera image with a cat, bear, or fox. Face tracking runs locally, and the camera track stores the avatar in cartoon mode.
- Choose picture-in-picture or either side-by-side layout from the camera toolbar before recording. Side-by-side layouts can fit the entire screen, and circular camera framing carries into the editor.
- Use models from connected local AI assistants for text tasks such as captions and script writing.
- Check, download, and install updates from About. The installer now includes the built-in wallpapers.

Requires macOS 13.3+ on Apple Silicon.
